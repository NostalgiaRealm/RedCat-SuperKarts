# Intentional differences from the original

The race is the original code; these are the places where the port shows or runs it differently on purpose.
Check this list before "fixing" a difference.

| Area | Original | Port | Why, and what it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screen shape | 640x480, 4:3 | Fills any shape. Wider: more at the sides at the same vertical framing (Hor+); taller: more above and below. At 4:3 it equals the original | Nothing in rcskspel.dat culls by aspect (static scan and a 6-track measurement, 2026-11-25), so no game code changes. The track's chunk window (55 degrees, `visiblechunks`) was tuned for 4:3: in the extra bands far track pops in a little more often than at 4:3 (mean 0.09% of cells at 16:9, 0.135% at 21:9, against 0.04%), and rain and snow, which the game spawns around the camera, thin out toward the sides as they already do toward the 4:3 edge |
| HUD | Laid out for 640x480 | Laid out as the JS reconstruction lays out its HUD (its `source/race-hud-layout.js`): each group is the 640x480 HUD at one scale in a frame at an edge or the centre, inside the safe area (notches, home bars; at least 8 CSS px at the top and sides of an upright screen) and above the touch controls. Portraits and stars top left, the race times top right, the bonus box bottom left and the minimap bottom right above the controls, the Tijdrace time and the start lights and arrows centred at the top. Upright, 480 of its 640 pixels span the width (the groups grow by 4/3 on a screen without side insets), the lights and arrows sit in the middle of the height and the stars 110 pixels lower, below the race times; in landscape the whole HUD fits the height above the controls and shrinks with them, except the race times. The notices are centred in the room left. Each group of meshes is cut to its frame as the JS reconstruction's viewport cuts it (portraits, stars, minimap, bonus box, start lights, arrows; not the strings): the start lights, hidden above their frame, come in at its top line, in the middle of an upright screen too. The bonus box is drawn into its frame as the JS reconstruction draws every group, so its model flies inside the frame from the frame's own start place, seen from the frame's centre. At 4:3 without insets nothing moves and nothing is cut. One place differs from the JS reconstruction: the sliding notice (Snelste ronde) still starts and ends as far off screen as at 4:3, where the JS reconstruction's centred frame lets it start inside a 21:9 screen | Done in game memory around the HUD's draw call 0x4614d0 and put back after it (`source/host/hud-layout.js`): no game code but the draw sees the moved positions. The cut: before each child of Screen, 0x458550 calls the child's draw callback +0x30 (0 in the HUD); while laid out it is a host thunk that tells the renderer the group's frame (a scissor, CMD.CLIP); the bonus box stays as at 4:3 in memory and the renderer draws it with its frame as the viewport and the game's 4:3 clip (CMD.CLIP mode 2), as exact for its 3D model as for flat parts. The arrows and stars, moved in memory at z 1.0005 and 1.001, are off the flat transform by 0.005 px. `tools/checks/hud-scale-check.mjs` compares every draw and its cut with the JS reconstruction's `raceHudLayout` |
| Finish flag | A sheet fixed to the camera, sized so that its edges never show at 4:3 | On other shapes the sheet grows evenly (its squares stay square) by the widening or heightening | Its edges would show at 16:9 and wider at every phase of its wave |
| 2D screens | Full screen | The quit requester and other full-screen 2D pictures are shown in the middle 4:3 area with black sides; the pointer maps through that area | They are 640x480 pictures |
| Smoothness | One picture per 25 ms pass (at most 40 a second) | The display shows a blend of the last two passes at its own rate, one pass (25 ms) behind the game. The HUD is not blended. `?nosmooth` shows the passes as they are | The game's state and its 25 ms passes are untouched; only what is shown between passes is new |
| Resolution, MSAA | The display mode's pixels, no anti-aliasing | Any drawing size and render scale, MSAA 2/4/8 | Picture only |
| Kart shadow (Schaduwen op het wegdek) | 0x4260c0 draws one S_SHADOW quad per racer with the kart's matrix, its Y on the sector's fallback plane (0x494b70, capped by the kart's Y); where that plane lies under the road (Frankrijk's start: Y 0, the road at 20 and more) the shadow is hidden, and where it crosses the road part of it is cut off. Its X/Z corners are the box of the mesh 0x490ce0 names for the kart object, its animation frame's `redcar.d3d` (28 x 37 units), which is never drawn: 0x4a0d80 draws the object's +0x99 mesh, a copy of `kart03.d3d` (41 x 64), so from the chase camera most of the quad lies under the kart | `settings.surfaceShadows` (on by default, as the JS reconstruction's; off: the original quad, untouched). An 8x8 grid over the box of the body the game draws (`kart03.d3d`, every racer and character; the JS reconstruction's footprint) with the original quad's world matrix (tilt, rescue shrink, +2 X/Z) whose points lie 0.25 above the ground the game's own triangle search finds under them (0x4977d0, 0x495fe0, 0x496290), drawn with a polygon offset and no depth writes, in its place before its kart, with the original texture, colour and blend states; blended between passes with its kart. Switches at race start and live after OK | The JS reconstruction's (kart-shadow.js:36-77, race-visuals.js:184-225). The game's code runs unchanged (the callback is wrapped, the draw replaced in the recorder; game memory identical on and off). The footprint follows the JS reconstruction (race-visuals.js:144 sizes its shadow by the body mesh's bounds, player-assets.js:65): the grid's corners are the drawn body's box record, bit for bit its kart03 quad, and its points match its surfaceKartShadowPositions bit for bit (`tests/shadows.test.mjs`); from the chase camera both darken the same road around the player (`tools/check-visuals.mjs js`, Frankrijk's start, RedCat waiting at the line: 1348 pixels against the JS reconstruction's 1454 where both show the road, overlap 0.93; at the start lights, pass 260, 1660 against 75 with the original footprint; 661 more of its darkened pixels lie under the recomp's rear wheels, which are the original's wide `wiela.d3d` where the JS reconstruction draws the narrow `wielv.d3d`). With the setting off the recomp keeps the original's redcar-sized quad, where the JS reconstruction draws its kart03-sized quad on the fallback plane too. Between grid points the shadow follows straight lines, so a kerb narrower than an eighth of the kart can show a gap. 0.03-0.06 ms per shadow in Node |
| Engine light (Motorlicht volgt de kart) | The flare (0x4888b0) is built and the kart drawn from the same place each pass | `settings.smoothEngineLights` (on by default, as the JS reconstruction's). On: each racer's flare is blended between passes with its kart (the same jump rule as the kart's own matrix), so it stays on the engine; off: shown at the newer pass while the kart is blended, so at speed it sits up to a pass ahead, inside the engine, as the JS reconstruction draws it off. Rocket flares always follow their rocket. Switches at race start and live after OK | Picture only. Before this the recomp cut a flare loose whenever its vertices moved more than 10 units a pass, two thirds of the time at racing speed. A kart turning more than 20 degrees in a pass keeps its newer rotation (the smoothing's wheel rule) while its flare stays blended point by point; no such turn in 4000 passes on Frankrijk and Duitsland |
| Input device | One device per race, chosen in the menu (race.ini CONTROL): keyboard, gamepad (digital) or joystick (analog); the keyboard does not drive in the other two | Keyboard, pad and touch together. The race starts in keyboard mode; before every pass the host writes the device word 0x53a5d8: the game's own analog joystick handler (0x4341d0) while the pad's stick or triggers or the touch wheel drive and no driving key is held, else the keyboard's ramps (0x433870). The pad and the touch wheel feed a virtual WinMM joystick that is always there, centred at boot (the game calibrates once). In the analog handler Ctrl and Alt count as the joystick's bonus and handbrake buttons. The pad: in the game's pause A and Start answer it (J, P), in a focus pause also B and the stick or D-pad (an arrow key for 80 ms), not X, Y, LB or RB; in the quit requester a focus from "nee" that the stick, D-pad or arrow keys move by putting the game's pointer on the other button's centre (SndGfx.ini [STOPPEN] 150,350 / 425,350, 115x38: 207,369 and 482,369, where 0x473820 itself parks it) and that A, Enter or Space press by holding the left mouse button there for 80 ms (0x474040 takes a held button), B is N; Start is not P there nor after it until let go; no Back. Only 0x42ee60, 0x42ef90 and 0x4325b0 read the device word, each in its own device (`evidence/checks/input-modes.txt`); the analog handler keeps the original's ±10% window and its jump to ±10 at the edge, and the page maps the pad's dead-zone edge onto it so the two do not add up. Still different: a keyboard bonus or handbrake key held next to the pad keeps the analog handler here (they are its buttons), where any key: source makes the JS port digital (its input.js:49-50), and so does a touch Rem or Bonus next to the pad; left and right worked at once by two sources both answer "down" here, where the JS port takes their difference. A pad alone cannot open the quit requester, in either port (the JS port's B is Esc only in its menus, input.js:161) |
