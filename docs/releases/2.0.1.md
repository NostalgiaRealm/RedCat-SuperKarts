# RedCat SuperKarts 2.0.1

A fix release for [2.0.0](2.0.0.md), the recompiled port. Saves, settings and the race itself are unchanged.

Download from [GitHub](https://github.com/NostalgiaRealm/RedCat-SuperKarts/releases/tag/v2.0.1) or
[GitLab](https://gitlab.com/Nostalgia_Realm/RedCat-SuperKarts/-/releases/v2.0.1), or play at
[games.nostalgiarealm.com/redcatsuperkarts](https://games.nostalgiarealm.com/redcatsuperkarts/). See the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Fixed

- **Stationary objects snapping out of place and back while racing.** The display blends the game's last two
  25 ms steps. An object without a counterpart in the step before (something coming into view, a growing skid mark,
  or one whose twin another draw had taken; about one every two steps) was drawn with the newer step's camera, up to
  a whole step ahead of everything around it, and jumped back one step later. It is now seen through the same
  blended camera as the rest of the picture. Objects that did not move are matched with their exact twin first and
  the others closest pairs first, so neighbouring puffs of smoke no longer swap places.

## Downloads

The same packages as 2.0.0: Linux x64 (AppImage, `.tar.gz`), Windows x64 (installer, `.zip`), macOS Intel and Apple
Silicon (`.dmg`, `.zip`), the source and the original-code reference, with `SHA256SUMS.txt` and the `integrity-*.json`
reports. Windows packages are unsigned; macOS packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized.

## Tested and not tested

- **Tested:** `tools/checks/pairing-probe.mjs` (Nederland and Frankrijk, 1,500 steps each: every stationary draw keeps
  its own twin, every draw without one is seen through the blended camera) and the renderer's checks (smoothing,
  shapes, kart shadows and engine lights) in headless Chromium; the website package and the Linux desktop build as for
  2.0.0.
- **Not tested:** the fix looked at by eye on a real screen; everything 2.0.0 lists as not tested.
