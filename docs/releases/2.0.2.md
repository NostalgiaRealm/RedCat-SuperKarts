# RedCat SuperKarts 2.0.2

A fix release for [2.0.0](2.0.0.md), the recompiled port. Saves, settings and the race itself are unchanged.

Download from [GitHub](https://github.com/NostalgiaRealm/RedCat-SuperKarts/releases/tag/v2.0.2) or
[GitLab](https://gitlab.com/Nostalgia_Realm/RedCat-SuperKarts/-/releases/v2.0.2), or play at
[games.nostalgiarealm.com/redcatsuperkarts](https://games.nostalgiarealm.com/redcatsuperkarts/). See the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Fixed

- **Kart wheels glitching at speed**, above all with a speed powerup on Normaal or Moeilijk. The display blends the
  game's last two 25 ms steps. A kart's wheels and driver were matched with the nearest draw of the same mesh over all
  karts, so in a pack a wheel took another kart's wheel, and when a turbo carried a kart further in a step than its
  wheels lie apart it took its own other wheel: it was drawn halfway between two wheels or cut loose from its body. A
  kart's body, wheels and driver are now matched only with the same kart's, by their place on the kart, and move
  together; a kart and the camera are cut loose only by a jump of more than 150 units, such as a rescue.

## Downloads

The same packages as 2.0.0: Linux x64 (AppImage, `.tar.gz`), Windows x64 (installer, `.zip`), macOS Intel and Apple
Silicon (`.dmg`, `.zip`), the source and the original-code reference, with `SHA256SUMS.txt` and the `integrity-*.json`
reports. Windows packages are unsigned; macOS packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized.

## Tested and not tested

- **Tested:** played on a real screen with a speed powerup; `tools/checks/wheel-probe.mjs` (Nederland on Moeilijk and
  Frankrijk on Normaal, 3,000 steps each: every wheel, driver and body draw pairs with its own kart's, none with another
  kart's or another wheel), the turbo, smoothing, kart-shadow, pairing and free-flight checks in headless Chromium, and
  the Linux desktop build's launch check.
- **Not tested:** everything 2.0.0 lists as not tested.
